Judder

A haptic pattern editor for iPhone. Support and privacy.

You build a chain of events on one timeline, play it on the device in your hand, then take it away as AHAP or as paste-ready Swift.

Nothing happens when I press play

Two things cause this, and neither is a fault in the app.

You are running on the Simulator

The iOS Simulator has no Taptic Engine. It draws every timeline correctly and plays the audio layer, but it cannot produce haptic output at all. Judder reports this in the Engine tab, under Hardware, where Core Haptics reads as unsupported. Run on a real iPhone.

System vibration is switched off

Check this path on the device:

Settings › Accessibility › Touch › Vibration

When that switch is off, iOS silences haptics for every app on the phone, including Judder. There is no API to read the setting, so no app can detect it or warn you about it.

Requirements

An iPhone with a Taptic Engine, running iOS 17 or later. Judder is portrait iPhone only. There is no iPad layout and no Mac version.

My patterns disappeared

Judder does not save anything. Patterns live in memory for the life of the session, so closing the app discards them. Export what you want to keep, either as an .ahap file or as Swift, before you leave.

Contact

Email craigtrim@gmail.com. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version, and say what you expected to feel and what happened instead. If it involves a specific pattern, exporting the AHAP and pasting it in makes the problem much faster to reproduce.

Privacy

Judder collects nothing.

It has no network code, no analytics, no accounts, and no crash reporting. Nothing you build is transmitted anywhere, because the app contains nothing that transmits. There is no server behind it.

Nothing is stored either, on the device or off it. The app requests no permissions and reads no personal data, no contacts, no location, no photos and no identifiers.

Files you export go where you send them, through the standard iOS share sheet. What happens after that is between you and whichever app you shared into.
